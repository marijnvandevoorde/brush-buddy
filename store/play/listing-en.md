## Full description  (max 4000 characters)

```
Brush Buddy turns the daily fight over toothbrushing into something a child
actually wants to finish. A cheerful cartoon tooth sits in the middle of the
screen, covered in little germs, and gets visibly cleaner and happier as your
child brushes. No ads, no account, no internet needed.

TWO MINUTES, PROPERLY SPLIT

Dentists ask for two minutes — but they also ask that the time be spread evenly,
which is the part almost everyone gets wrong. Brush Buddy walks through the
mouth in six steps and holds each one for as long as it deserves:

• Top right, top left, bottom left, bottom right — 25 seconds each, because the
  molars at the back are where brushing is usually rushed.
• Top front and bottom front — 10 seconds each. They are easy to reach and do
  not need a full turn.

That is exactly 120 seconds. A ring of teeth around the buddy lights up to show
where to brush right now, and a progress arc fills as the seconds tick down.

IT PROMPTS FOR THE SURFACES TOO

Within each section the app cycles through the surfaces most children forget:
outsides, chewing tops, and insides — with a short written cue and a gentle tick
each time it moves on. Front teeth get outsides and insides; the back teeth get
the chewing surfaces as well.

THE GERMS COME OFF

Every section starts with germs scattered across the buddy's tooth. They pop and
vanish as that part of the mouth gets its time, so a child can see the progress
rather than just being told about it. The buddy's face brightens as the mouth
gets cleaner.

A REWARD WORTH FINISHING FOR

Reach the end and the buddy's chosen character bursts onto the screen to
celebrate — a tooth fairy, a girl or boy dentist, or a girl or boy superhero.
Your child picks which one, and it is genuinely the reason they will come back
tomorrow.

A STREAK THAT BUILDS THE HABIT

A small pill at the top counts consecutive days brushed and the stars earned
along the way. It is quiet encouragement, not pressure — nothing is ever lost or
taken away.

MADE TO SUIT YOUR CHILD

• Nine colour schemes, from candy pink to calm teal.
• Five characters to choose between.
• A calm mode that turns off the buzzing and the livelier animation, for
  children who find that too much.
• Sound on or off, with soft cues rather than jingles.
• Four languages: English, Dutch, French and Spanish.

FOR PARENTS

• No advertising, ever.
• No in-app purchases. You pay once and that is it.
• No account, no sign-in, no email address.
• No tracking and no analytics. The app collects nothing at all.
• No internet connection required — the app does not even ask for internet
  permission, so it works in a bathroom with no signal, or on a tablet that has
  never been online.
• No outbound links, no social media, no chat.

The streak, the stars and your settings are stored on the device and nowhere
else. The screen stays awake for the whole two minutes so nobody has to touch a
wet phone mid-brush.
```
